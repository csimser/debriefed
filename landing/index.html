Translate Your Service Into a Civilian Career

Debriefed turns evals, MOS descriptions, and performance write-ups into civilian resume language, builds cover letters, tailors resumes to job descriptions, and shows your skill gaps. It runs entirely on your own computer — no account, no internet required after download.

Download Debriefed One HTML file. Double-click it and it opens in your browser.  ·  View on GitHub

Dictionary Mode

Free, no setup. A built-in 33,000+ entry military translation dictionary powers everything — resume building, bullet translation, job matching, and cover letters — fully offline. No account, no API key, no cost.

AI-Enhanced Mode

Optional, ~2–6¢ per resume. Add your own Anthropic API key in Settings and Claude polishes the dictionary output and reads PDFs. You pay Anthropic directly — Debriefed never sees your key or your data.

No accounts. No servers. No tracking. Your profile and resumes live only in your browser, on your device — export them to a file anytime.
